Frequently Asked Questions

How does it work?
Your news outlet subscribes to the service and chooses the writers and editors who will have access to the app. We work with the outlet to upload its style guide to the app, only accessible to that outlet’s employees. The app marks style errors in the copy provided, and users approve or reject the fixes.
How much does it cost?
Pricing is custom, dependent on the size of the outlet. Contact us for an estimate.
Can we try the service before we commit?
Yes, you can sign up for a seven-day free trial.
How much will the app manipulate the text?
The app is designed to edit ONLY for AP style and your outlet’s own style guide. It highlights each fix and explains the relevant style guide rule. It doesn’t check facts or refine copy beyond editing for style rules.
